SCIN eFST CHECK OF nn_colorimetry_dermatoscopy
Prepared 28 September 2026.


1. WHAT THIS IS

A check of the released Marin Benčević colorimetry models on 200 public SCIN
images: 100 with dermatologist-estimated Fitzpatrick (eFST) 1–3 and 100 with
eFST 4–6. The models were not modified, fine-tuned, or masked. This is a
reference run, not a validation of our auditor.

Source images: gs://dx-scin-public-data (SCIN public bucket).
Models: github.com/marinbenc/nn_colorimetry_dermatoscopy, release tag preprint.
Per-image output: scin_efst_colorimetry_results.csv
Sample list: nn_colorimetry_dermatoscopy/data/scin/sample_manifest.csv


2. WHAT WAS LEFT UNCHANGED

Both released heads were used as published.

  finetune_mskcc_blur0       EfficientNet-B4, CORAL ordinal head, Fitzpatrick
  finetune_mskcc_blur0_lab   EfficientNet-B4, CIELab regression, used for ITA

Five folds each. Weights were loaded from the release and not trained further.
Preprocessing matches the fine-tune configs: resize to 224×224, ImageNet
normalization, blur off, white balance off. No lesion mask. The Fitzpatrick
call is the majority vote of the five folds. ITA is the mean Lab prediction
across folds, then arctan2(L − 50, b) in degrees, which is the formula in
the repo's test_lab.py.

These networks were trained on dermoscopic images with colorimeter labels.
SCIN images are clinical photographs. That domain gap is the main limit on
how to read the numbers below.


3. HOW THE 200 IMAGES WERE CHOSEN

eFST is the majority of dermatologist_fitzpatrick_skin_type_label_1/2/3.
Cases with a tie, or with no parseable FST label, were left out. Self-reported
Fitzpatrick was not used. Image-quality and "gradable for Fitzpatrick" flags
were not used as filters.

Each bucket is a uniform random sample of 100 distinct images from every SCIN
image on a qualifying case. Seed 42. Shot type was not filtered. A case can
contribute more than one of its images.

Pools before the draw:
  eFST 1–3    6,273 images
  eFST 4–6    2,309 images

What landed in the sample:

  eFST    count     shot type              count
  1         16      close-up                 96
  2         40      at an angle              55
  3         44      at a distance            49
  4         63
  5         35      unique cases, eFST 1–3   97
  6          2      unique cases, eFST 4–6   99

Most cases have a single dermatologist eFST label (83 of 100 in the light
bucket, 87 of 100 in the dark bucket). Type 6 is almost absent in this draw
because it is rare in the public set, not because it was excluded.

The two buckets separate in the expected direction: lighter eFST has higher
ITA. The absolute values do not. Published ITA bands for Fitzpatrick I–III
sit well above these means (often above +20, and type I above +55). A mean
near 0 for eFST 1–3 is not a calibrated colorimeter reading of those photos.
Type 6 has only two images, so that row is not a rate.


6. HOW TO READ THIS

The model ranks the two SCIN buckets, and it is usually within one Fitzpatrick
type of the dermatologist label. It is not calibrated as an ITA meter on
clinical photos, and it systematically calls light skin darker.

Two setup facts explain part of that, and neither was changed for this test.
The training images are dermoscopy, not phone photos. Inference uses the whole
frame, so lesion, background, and clothing stay in the input. Our own rule is
to measure ITA on healthy skin after the lesion is masked, and to abstain when
too little skin remains. This run did not do that.

Do not treat these 200 images as a test set for our auditor. They were drawn
to see what this released checkpoint does on SCIN.
